# Data Storm 7.0 - Finale Guidelines

**An Initiative by:** Rotaract Clubs of Faculty of Science, University of Colombo and University of Moratuwa  
**Powered by:** OCTAVE  

---

## CONGRATULATIONS!
Congratulations on being selected as one of the Top 10 Finalist Teams of Data Storm 7.0. Your team has successfully progressed through the Storming Round and Prototype Phase and is now invited to present your solution at the Grand Finale. This document outlines the presentation requirements, evaluation criteria and submission guidelines.

## GRAND FINALE OBJECTIVE
The Grand Finale is designed to evaluate not only the technical excellence of your solution but also its business viability, practical impact, innovation, and presentation effectiveness. Teams are expected to present their end-to-end solution as a professional business pitch supported by strong technical foundations.

## PRESENTATION FORMAT
Each team will be allocated:
* **Presentation:** 10 Minutes
* **Live system demonstration:** 5 Minutes

*(Followed by a Question & Answer Session)*

Teams are expected to cover the business pitch with technical aspects, system demonstration and implementation within the allocated time. Teams must strictly adhere to the allocated time. Presentations exceeding the allocated time may be interrupted.

## MANDATORY DELIVERABLES
Each team should prepare:

### A. Presentation Deck
A professional slide presentation covering:
1. Problem Statement
2. Business Understanding
3. Dataset Overview
4. Data Preparation
5. Exploratory Data Analysis
6. Methodology
7. Model Development
8. Model Evaluation
9. Key Insights
10. Business Impact
11. Solution Architecture
12. Streamlit Application Demonstration
13. Future Improvements
14. Conclusion

### B. Final Business Pitch
The presentation should focus on:
* Why the problem matters
* How your solution addresses it
* Value generated by your solution
* Business feasibility
* Practical implementation

### C. Working Prototype
Teams should be prepared to demonstrate:
* Streamlit Application
* Core functionalities
* User workflow
* Key outputs and insights

## JUDGING CONSIDERATIONS
The judging panel will likely assess teams based on:

**Technical Excellence**
* Quality of data processing
* Model selection and implementation
* Performance evaluation

**Innovation**
* Creativity of approach
* Novel use of AI/Data Science techniques

**Business Impact**
* Practical applicability
* Value creation
* Feasibility of implementation

**Prototype Quality**
* Functionality
* User experience
* Deployment quality

**Presentation Quality**
* Storytelling
* Clarity
* Professionalism
* Ability to answer questions

## Q&A SESSION
Judges may ask questions regarding:
* Dataset selection
* Data preprocessing
* Feature engineering
* Model architecture
* Evaluation metrics
* Business applicability
* Deployment decisions
* Scalability
* Ethical considerations

*Every team member should be prepared to answer questions.*

## PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
Teams are expected to:
* Dress professionally.
* Maintain professional communication.
* Respect allocated time limits.
* Ensure all presented work is original.

## FINAL REMINDER
The Grand Finale is not only about achieving the highest model accuracy. Teams should demonstrate:
* Strong technical foundations
* Clear business value
* Effective communication
* Practical implementation
* Impactful storytelling

We wish all finalist teams the very best for the Data Storm 7.0 Grand Finale.
